# Chemistry Curiosity Parking Lot

A lightweight record of worthwhile questions that arise during lessons but would take us beyond the current learning objective.

**Policy:** See [Learning Style and Teaching Approach — Scope management](learning-approach.md#13-scope-management--balanced-exploration).

## How this works

- **Core:** Explore in the current lesson.
- **Adjacent:** Briefly clarify and return to the lesson.
- **Beyond scope:** Offer a short orientation, park the deeper question here, and return.
- Only record substantive deferred questions; don't add every passing aside.
- Revisit entries when the roadmap naturally reaches the relevant material, at unit boundaries, or when the learner chooses.
- Do not assess parked topics as though they were taught.
- When revisiting, update the status and include a lesson or study-record reference if available.
